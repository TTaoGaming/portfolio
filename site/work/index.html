What I have built and explored

I work across movement, interaction, and reliable software. This page separates things you can try now from the larger systems I am still developing.

01 / Spatial interaction

Turning movement into input

My Spatial Input work connects camera observations to browser actions through normalization, filtering, intent, and a stable event interface. The ambition is to let different kinds of input drive an experience without rebuilding the experience for every sensor. The complete multi-input system remains an engineering direction; the demos below show narrower pieces of it.

Try it · public alpha · webcam

Signal Refinery / HandPiano ↗

Hands-free piano using browser hand tracking, a kinematic guard, dwell, and hitboxes. A desktop browser and webcam are recommended. The demo labels its tuning work in progress.

Prototype family · not a public release

Pointer, games, and music controls

Other experiments include a gesture-to-pointer bridge, table-tennis interaction, and hand-to-audio controls. I use them to test primitives such as tracking stability, dwell, impact, and continuous control. Public artifacts for these prototypes are still being selected.

02 / Playable browser work

Small experiences, direct feedback

Try it · live browser hub

Omega Games ↗

A collection of small, tap-to-play HTML5 games. These use regular touch and mouse input; they are a separate part of my work from the webcam demo.

03 / AI-agent reliability

Checking the claim of “done”

As I built software with AI agents, I saw recurring errors: work reported as complete before a test ran, drafts described as sent, and self-review that missed the same failure as the original work. This became a separate line of tools and writing about independently checkable outcomes.

Inspect the source · open tools

AgentReleaseGate OSS ↗

An open policy and examples for checking AI-agent claims. The repository includes a claim gate, a reward-hack mirror, and a free field guide.

Read · companion site

Worldweaver ↗

The AI/swarm-facing side of my work: cards, public context, dated world-state views, and regeneration paths. Its observations have their own dates and limits.
